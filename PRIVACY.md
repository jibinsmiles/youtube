# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

**Tech Tadka Robot** is a personal automation tool, operated by its owner (jibinsmiles), that creates videos and uploads
them to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

## What the app accesses
- Permission to upload videos, thumbnails and metadata to YouTube channels owned by the person who authorised the app.
- Nothing else. It does not read private videos, comments, subscribers or any other account data beyond what is
  needed to upload and confirm the upload.

## What is stored
- An OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub Actions settings, used only to upload.
- A record of which news topics and video IDs were already covered, stored in this repository (no personal data).

## Sharing
The app does not sell, share or disclose Google user data to anyone. The app is used only by its owner.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Contact
Open an issue at https://github.com/jibinsmiles/youtube/issues.
